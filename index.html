Privacy Policy

Study4TOEIC
Effective date: May 22, 2026

Short version: Study4TOEIC does not collect, sell, share, or track your personal data. Study progress is stored on your device.

Information We Collect

Study4TOEIC does not collect personal information from users. The app does not require an account, login, name, email address, phone number, or location.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores study-related information locally on your device so the app can remember your progress. This may include:

  • Vocabulary study progress
  • Completed lessons
  • Quiz history and scores
  • Study streaks
  • Selected study language
  • Notification reminder time

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Study4TOEIC uses local notifications to remind you to study. Notification permission is optional and can be changed at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Speech and Audio

Study4TOEIC uses device speech and audio features for English pronunciation and quiz sound effects. The app does not record your voice.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Study4TOEIC does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Study4TOEIC is an education app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your local study data inside the app by opening Settings and choosing the reset option. You can also delete the app from your device to remove its local data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
